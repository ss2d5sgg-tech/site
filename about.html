How we check fares

FareWise Japan makes free calculators for travellers in Japan. We are a small team based in Japan.

Where the numbers come from

  • Pass prices and rules come from the issuing operator's official website: JR West, Kintetsu, Osaka Metro, Kyoto City Bus & Subway and others.
  • We read the Japanese pages, not just the English ones. Discontinued passes and price changes often appear there first.
  • Single fares come from the operators' official fare information.

How often we check

Every number carries a source link and a "checked on" date. We re-check all of them at least once a month and after any announced price change.

How we use AI

We use AI tools to help read official pages and to build the calculators. A person reviews every page before it goes live, and every number is tied to an official source you can open yourself.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish reviews or testimonials we can't verify.
  • We don't rank options by commission. The calculator shows the cheapest option for your trip, whether or not we earn anything from it.

Found an outdated number? Please tell us so we can fix it.
