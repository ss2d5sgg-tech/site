    How the calculator works

    • For each day we take the cheapest way to make each journey, then try every start day and length for each pass, and keep the cheapest total.
    • Day trips are counted as a return journey from your hotel city. We assume you can reach either the JR or the private-railway station without an extra fare. If you can't, add a Metro ride.
    • Osaka city days use Osaka Metro (¥190–390 a ride; we use ¥240 unless you change it). Kyoto city days use the ¥230 city bus. We compare these with the Enjoy Eco Card (¥820 weekdays, ¥620 weekends and holidays) and the Kyoto Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass (¥1,100). We count Saturdays and Sundays as weekends; national holidays are not included.
    • Not included: Shinkansen, limited-express surcharges other than HARUKA, attraction passes such as the Osaka Amazing Pass, and child fares.
    • Passes marked "visitor only" require a foreign passport and Temporary Visitor entry status.
