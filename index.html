How we keep the numbers right

  1. Official sources only

    Every fare and pass price comes from the operator's own page, usually the Japanese one, which is often more complete than the English version.

  2. Dated and linked

    Each number shows where it came from and when we last checked it.

  3. Re-checked monthly

    Passes get discontinued and prices change. We re-check every number at least once a month.
